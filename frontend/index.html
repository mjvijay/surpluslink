Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

How does SurplusLink ensure food safety?

Every listing requires a strict pickup deadline in the future and at most 48 hours away. Each card displays a real-time freshness bar that shifts from green to amber to chili red as the deadline approaches, and unclaimed listings automatically expire the moment time runs out.

Are phone numbers visible to everyone on the internet?

No. Phone numbers are strictly hidden on public listings. Contact numbers are revealed only after a registered receiver claims a listing, and only to the specific donor and receiver involved in that pickup (plus platform administrators).

What happens if two volunteers click "Claim" at the exact same moment?

Our backend uses an atomic database guard (`UPDATE ... WHERE status='available'`). Only the first request succeeds, and the second user immediately receives a polite notification that the listing was just claimed.

What if a volunteer claims a listing but cannot make it?

Receivers can click "Release Claim" from the Live Board or My Activity page at any time before pickup, immediately returning the listing to "Available" so another NGO can rescue it.
